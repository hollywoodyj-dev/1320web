# The 1320 Brand Bible

**The Canonical Language & Brand Expression Standard**

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Foundational Document
- **Authority:** Official Brand & Language Standard
- **Publisher:** SeeSoul Psychotherapy

---

## 1. Purpose

The 1320 Brand is experienced primarily through language.

Every report, webpage, email, Wisewave API response, Personal Integration Session, and future product should sound as though they come from one coherent source.

The purpose of this document is to preserve that consistency.

---

## 2. Brand Identity

1320 is **not** positioned as:

- an expert
- a guru
- a spiritual authority
- a fortune teller
- a diagnostic system

1320 **is** positioned as:

- a symbolic reflection framework
- a language for awareness
- a structured mirror
- a trusted companion for self-understanding

Customers should **never** feel: *"The system knows me."*

Customers should feel: *"The system helps me understand myself."*

---

## 3. Brand Personality

**1320 is:** Calm, thoughtful, grounded, warm, precise, reflective, editorial, emotionally mature.

**1320 is never:** Sensational, fear-driven, dogmatic, grandiose, mystical for its own sake, overly therapeutic, sales-driven.

---

## 4. Brand Voice

The 1320 voice is:

| Is | Is not |
|----|--------|
| Invitational | Commanding |
| Reflective | Absolute |
| Confident | Arrogant |
| Curious | Certain |
| Supportive | Dependent |

Every sentence should create space rather than pressure.

---

## 5. Brand Promise

**1320 promises:** Awareness, reflection, integration, clarity, personal responsibility.

**1320 never promises:** Prediction, certainty, guaranteed transformation, manifestation, destiny, perfect relationships, financial success.

---

## 6. Language Principles

Every customer-facing sentence should move toward:

```text
Awareness → Reflection → Understanding → Choice → Integration
```

Never toward:

```text
Fear → Dependency → Authority → Certainty → Obedience
```

---

## 7. Canonical Vocabulary

### Core Language (Preferred)

Awareness, Reflection, Mirror, Pattern, Choice, Agency, Integration, Practice, Growth, Meaning, Contribution, Receiving, Relationship Mirror, Growth Rhythm, Soul Sovereignty, Return to Source.

### Deprecated Public Language

Do not use publicly:

- Who You Attract
- Money Frequency
- Spiritual Maturity
- Higher Frequency
- Low Frequency
- Soulmate Match
- Compatibility Score
- Energy Ranking
- Raw Value
- Destined Partner
- Financial Destiny

### Internal Language

Allowed only inside: Engineering, QA, Resolver, Calculation, Documentation.

Examples: Resolver Key, Modulo, Raw Value, Calculation Index, Database ID, Internal Code.

These terms should **never** appear in customer-facing products.

---

## 8. Sentence Style

**Preferred:**

- "This may reflect..."
- "You may notice..."
- "One possible pattern is..."
- "This can invite..."
- "This layer often appears when..."
- "This is an invitation rather than a conclusion."

**Avoid:**

- "You are..."
- "This proves..."
- "You always..."
- "You will..."
- "This guarantees..."
- "You should..."
- "This is your destiny."

---

## 9. Emotional Tone

The customer should feel: Safe, curious, grounded, respected, capable.

Never: Judged, fearful, dependent, inferior, broken, chosen, superior.

---

## 10. Reflection Style

Good questions begin with: What, Where, How, When, Which.

Good questions **never** begin with: Why are you..., Why can't you..., Shouldn't you..., Don't you...

Reflection should expand awareness rather than defend identity.

---

## 11. Brand Narrative

The customer journey follows one emotional progression:

```text
Recognition → Understanding → Acceptance → Integration → Action → Return
```

Every product should reinforce this narrative.

---

## 12. Writing Standards

Every piece of copy should feel: Human-written, editorial, purposeful, intentional, readable, elegant, concise.

Never: Mechanically assembled, overwritten, overly spiritual, AI-generated, repetitive, sales-heavy.

---

## 13. Wisewave API Language

Wisewave API represents the living voice of the 1320 Platform.

Every response should:

- Preserve agency
- Invite reflection
- Connect report patterns
- Remain emotionally grounded
- Respect uncertainty
- Avoid prediction, authority, dependency

Wisewave extends the Brand Voice into conversation. It does not replace it.

---

## 14. Personal Integration Language

**Facilitators should:** Ask, reflect, clarify, summarise, connect, invite.

**Facilitators should not:** Interpret as absolute truth, predict outcomes, diagnose, give life decisions, replace professional care.

The report remains the foundation. The conversation supports integration.

---

## 15. Marketing Language

Marketing should inspire curiosity. Never urgency.

**Use:** Discover, Explore, Understand, Reflect, Recognise, Notice.

**Avoid:** Unlock your destiny, Transform instantly, Secret knowledge, Ancient truth, Guaranteed change.

---

## 16. Quality Checklist

Before publishing any content ask:

- ✓ Does it sound like 1320?
- ✓ Does it preserve agency?
- ✓ Does it avoid prediction?
- ✓ Does it avoid certainty?
- ✓ Would the same sentence work inside a report?
- ✓ Would Wisewave API speak this way?
- ✓ Would a facilitator say this naturally?
- ✓ Does it reinforce trust?

If not, rewrite before publishing.

---

## 17. Brand Consistency

Every public surface should sound like one conversation:

Homepage, Reports, Checkout, Emails, Wisewave API, Personal Integration Sessions, Future Apps, Future Products.

Customers should never feel they are talking to different brands.

---

## Closing Statement

The 1320 Brand is not defined by its logo, colours, or typography.

The 1320 Brand is defined by how people feel after reading a single sentence.

Every word should help people become more aware of themselves.

Never more dependent on the system.

---

## Brand Principle

Language is not decoration.

Language is behaviour.

Behaviour becomes trust.

Trust becomes the brand.

Every word written within the 1320 Platform should strengthen that trust.
