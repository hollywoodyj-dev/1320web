## S6-01 · The Clear Self-Worth Receiver / 清晰价值接收者

### Reflection Question

How does my value want to be recognized, received, and grounded in real life?

### 1. Value Essence / 价值本质

This S6 pattern reflects value that grows through clarity, authenticity, and the courage to be seen. It does not define income or financial destiny; it mirrors how the person may recognize value, offer it clearly, and allow support to return without self-abandonment.

### 2. What Your Soul Is Learning to Receive / 灵魂正在学习接收什么

The soul is learning to receive from alignment rather than pressure. Receiving becomes cleaner when clarity is trusted as a real form of value instead of something that must be hidden, over-proven, or over-explained.

### 3. How Value Wants to Flow / 价值如何流动

Value wants to flow when the person turns their natural gift into grounded expression. The old money-language source described this pattern through the relationship between value and wealth; in the current system, NOVA should translate it as a symbolic receiving pattern, not a financial prediction.

### 4. Natural Value Fields / 自然价值场域

service, creative contribution, leadership, teaching, conscious business

### 5. Shadow Distortion of Receiving / 接收的阴影扭曲

When distorted by fear, this pattern may move into hiding, feeling not enough, or waiting for validation. The person may block receiving by proving, withholding, over-controlling, over-giving, or waiting until everything feels completely safe before allowing value to circulate.

### 6. Mature Receiving Expression / 成熟接收方式

The mature expression appears when the person lets value be visible, practical, and reciprocal. They can give without disappearing, receive without guilt, and create structures where their contribution is respected rather than silently consumed.

### 7. Wisewave Reflection / 玄微引导语

当你不再隐藏你的光，财富就会出现。

### Legacy Source Note

- Money Core Frequency: 财富来自你的本质清晰度。你越忠于自己的真我，财富越自然呈现。

- Shadow Frequency: 怕被看见、怕不够好、价值感不足导致的自我设限。

- Soul–Wealth Relationship: 你的财富会随着你的真实表达能力而扩大。当你越真实，丰盛越靠近。

- Karmic Money Lesson: 学习承认自己的价值，而不是等待外界肯定。

### Safe Language Note

This interpretation is symbolic and reflective. It is not financial advice, income prediction, investment guidance, diagnostic language, or a statement of fixed identity.
