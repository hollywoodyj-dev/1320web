# C-4 · Claim Fidelity Check

All derivative publishing (Pinterest pins, ads creative, carousels, thumbnails, captions, cut-downs) follows one pipeline:

```text
Canonical Source → Claim Ledger → Visual Derivatives
```

There are not two content systems. A pin is a derivative of a ledgered claim, which is a derivative of a canonical source (page, report layer, or research artifact). If the source is not ready, the derivative is not ready.

## Gate — four questions, any Yes = Do not publish

Ask all four before every derivative ships. The first three catch invention, intensification, and dropped limits. The fourth catches a quieter Pinterest failure mode: a reflective invitation compressed into an identity.

1. **Did we invent a claim the canonical source does not make?**
2. **Did we strengthen the claim** (resonance or tradition presented as evidence or authority)?
3. **Did we drop a boundary, disclaimer, or limit of interpretation?**
4. **Did reflection become identity?**  
   Did “this symbol invites you to notice X” get compressed into “this number means you are X”?

If any answer is **Yes → Do not publish.**

Question 4 is the one the first three will miss. It is the drift most likely to happen silently on Pinterest.

## Naming vs asserting (T26 language standard)

**Naming a symbol is allowed.** **Asserting the symbol as an independently established reality is not.**

The goal is not to make 1320 sound uncertain about its own framework. The goal is a precise distinction between a symbol the system intentionally uses and a fact the system claims to have established.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| 1320 calls this Soul Mission. | This is your true Soul Mission. |
| 1320 uses the term original pattern. | S1 reveals your original self. |
| Awakening Gate is symbolic language within the framework. | Your Void Gate shows that an awakening is occurring. |

Reuse across S0–S9 copy and all derivatives checked through this gate.
