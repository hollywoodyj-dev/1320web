# The 1320 Constitution

**The Foundational Constitution of the 1320 Product System**

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Foundational Document
- **Authority:** Highest-Level Governance Document

---

## Preamble

The purpose of the 1320 Product System is not to tell people who they are.

Its purpose is to provide a structured symbolic framework through which people may observe themselves with greater clarity, awareness, and personal responsibility.

Everything built within the 1320 ecosystem—including reports, digital products, future Wisewave API services, and human-facilitated experiences—must remain faithful to this purpose.

This Constitution defines the principles that govern the evolution of the entire 1320 platform.

No future feature, technology, marketing initiative, or service should contradict these principles.

---

## Article I — The Nature of 1320

1320 is a symbolic self-reflection system.

It exists to support awareness.

It does not exist to provide certainty.

1320 offers language.

It does not impose identity.

1320 presents patterns.

It does not determine destiny.

---

## Article II — The Authority Principle

The user always remains the final authority over their own life.

Neither the 1320 reports, nor the Wisewave API, nor future facilitators, nor any future artificial intelligence implementation, may replace the user's own judgment, responsibility, or freedom of choice.

The role of the system is reflection.

Never authority.

---

## Article III — The Purpose Principle

Every feature within the 1320 ecosystem must serve at least one of the following purposes:

- Increase awareness.
- Encourage reflection.
- Support integration.
- Improve understanding.
- Strengthen personal agency.

Features that primarily create dependency, certainty, fear, or authority should not become part of the product.

---

## Article IV — Product Integrity

There is only one 1320 product.

Different platforms may present that product differently.

They must not redefine it.

Website, Mobile, PDF, Wisewave API, Personal Integration Sessions, future applications, and future partner integrations must all represent the same philosophy.

Presentation may change.

Meaning may not.

---

## Article V — Language Integrity

Language is part of the product.

Every customer-facing word must reinforce:

- awareness
- reflection
- agency
- humility
- openness

Language must never intentionally create:

- fear
- dependence
- superiority
- spiritual hierarchy
- false certainty

The Canonical Vocabulary Guide governs all public language.

---

## Article VI — Ethical Boundaries

The 1320 Product System must never present itself as:

- fortune telling
- prophecy
- diagnosis
- therapy
- financial advice
- legal advice
- investment advice
- medical advice
- spiritual authority

The system supports reflection.

Professional decisions remain outside its scope.

---

## Article VII — The Wisewave Principle

Wisewave is the intelligence layer of the 1320 Product System.

Its purpose is to help users explore the meaning already contained within the 1320 framework.

**Wisewave may:**

- explain
- connect
- reflect
- clarify
- integrate
- ask meaningful questions

**Wisewave must never:**

- predict
- command
- diagnose
- manipulate
- replace human responsibility
- invent report content
- claim certainty

Behaviour is governed by the Wisewave Behaviour Standard.

---

## Article VIII — Product Promise

Every customer should receive exactly what has been promised.

Marketing, Website, Checkout, Email, Report, Wisewave, and future services must communicate one consistent experience.

Trust is maintained through consistency.

---

## Article IX — Product Evolution

1320 should evolve through refinement rather than expansion.

New products should deepen understanding.

They should not create disconnected systems.

Future developments should extend one coherent ecosystem.

---

## Article X — Release Governance

Every public release must pass the official Release Gates.

- **Gate 0** — Calculation & Database Truth
- **Gate 1** — Product Experience
- **Gate 2** — Product & Ethical Language
- **Gate 3** — Full Report Integrity
- **Gate 4** — Behaviour Safety
- **Gate 5** — Commerce & Delivery

No public release should bypass these governance standards.

---

## Article XI — Stewardship

Every contributor shares responsibility for protecting the integrity of the 1320 Product System.

- Engineering protects correctness.
- Design protects experience.
- Content protects meaning.
- Quality Assurance protects consistency.
- Wisewave protects behaviour.
- Product Leadership protects vision.

No discipline stands above another.

Each safeguards a different aspect of the same product.

---

## Article XII — Change Governance

Changes affecting any of the following require formal review:

- calculation logic
- symbolic framework
- customer-facing terminology
- ethical boundaries
- report structure
- product promises
- Wisewave behaviour
- release criteria

Every approved change should be documented with:

- version number
- rationale
- impacted systems
- required regression gates

The integrity of the platform is preserved through transparent change management.

---

## Closing Principle

The success of the 1320 Product System will not be measured by how much certainty it provides.

It will be measured by whether people leave with greater clarity, greater responsibility, and greater freedom than when they arrived.

Every report. Every conversation. Every future product. Every line of code.

Should strengthen that outcome.

---

## Foundational Statement

1320 does not exist to become the voice people follow.

It exists to help people hear their own more clearly.

That principle stands above every feature, every technology, and every future evolution of the platform.
